Problem Statement
Problem Statement

Build a Machine Learning model that predicts whether a bank customer is likely to leave the bank (churn) based on customer information such as:

Credit score
Geography
Gender
Age
Balance
Number of products
Active status
Estimated salary

The goal is to help banks:

  Reduce customer loss
  Improve customer retention
  Identify risky customers early

  Objective

Banks lose revenue when customers leave.

This project helps:

  Identify customers likely to churn
  Improve retention strategies
  Increase customer satisfaction
  Reduce business loss

  Outcomes
Outcome 1

Older customers are more likely to churn.

Outcome 2

Inactive members have higher churn rate.

Outcome 3

Customers with fewer products may leave more.

Outcome 4

Customers with low engagement are risky.

Outcome 5

Geography affects churn patterns.
